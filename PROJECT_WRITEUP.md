# FrostBite Chargeback Risk Predictor — Methodology & Findings

The application analyses a reproducible synthetic dataset of 3,000 transactions across a mature six-month cohort, six European countries, four payment methods and four digital-goods categories. Its explainable heuristic assigns each transaction a 0–100 score using payment method, first-time-buyer status, amount above €100, country, product category and account age, with operations users able to tune the relative weights. Each score includes human-readable contributing factors, and the highest-scoring 10% are placed in a manual-review queue. The synthetic generator intentionally makes first-time buyers, card transactions, higher-value purchases, in-game currency and selected country cohorts riskier so that analysts can explore known patterns. Chargeback outcomes are assigned probabilistically and occur 10–90 days after the original transaction, allowing the dashboard to show time-to-chargeback distributions. The dashboard compares rates by payment method, country and product category and supports date and country filters with daily or weekly trends. The simulator estimates review workload, potential prevented disputes, analyst cost and net financial impact under user-controlled assumptions. All displayed patterns are generated for demonstration and must not be interpreted as actual FrostBite customer behaviour or as evidence that a country or product is inherently risky. Before production use, the model should be calibrated on matured real outcomes, evaluated out of time, checked for fairness and monitored alongside human decisions.
